Dear Editors:

We submit "Sparse Public Surveillance Limits Forecasting of Reported
Hantavirus Incidence, European Union and European Economic Area, 2019-2023" for
consideration as a Research article in *Emerging Infectious Diseases*.

This manuscript reports a reproducible public-surveillance benchmark for
reported hantavirus incidence in EU/EEA countries during 2019-2023. The
public-health contribution is not a deployable prediction system. The
contribution is a calibration-aware evaluation showing that sparse annual
public data do not support strong covariate-driven forecasting claims and that
simple surveillance-history baselines remain difficult to beat.

The submission package includes an EID-style model-input table listing sources,
ranges, missingness rules, and inclusion logic; a model schematic; sensitivity
analyses for surveillance-quality caveats and COVID-era training years;
leave-one-country-out influence analyses; calibration-localization tables; and
a simulation-based detectability screen. Together, these analyses support the
central conclusion that standardized surveillance metadata and calibrated
uncertainty reporting are prerequisites for credible pan-European annual
hantavirus forecasting from public data.

The manuscript does not claim operational risk prediction, causal environmental
drivers, individual-level risk, within-country risk, or generalizability beyond
EU/EEA annual reported surveillance. All analyses are retrospective. No
individual-level, clinical, address-level, or restricted data were used;
institutional review was not required.

All analysis code, processed public-data tables, and reproducibility scripts are
archived at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20150542. Before final submission,
the DOI should be verified against the final clean archive version. Source data
are from ECDC, World Bank, FAOSTAT, TerraClimate, and Natural Earth.

Artificial intelligence tools were used to assist with manuscript planning,
language editing, and pre-submission quality checks. The author verified all
analyses, references, and claims and accepts responsibility for the final
manuscript. No AI-generated figures were used.

The manuscript has not been submitted elsewhere. The author has no competing
interests and received no external funding for this work. The author confirms
approval of the final manuscript.

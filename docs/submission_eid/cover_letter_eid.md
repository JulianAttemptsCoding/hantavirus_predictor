# Cover Letter for Emerging Infectious Diseases

May 14, 2026

To the Editors,
*Emerging Infectious Diseases*

We plan to submit "Sparse Public Surveillance Limits Forecasting of Reported
Hantavirus Incidence, European Union and European Economic Area, 2019-2023" for
consideration as a Research article in *Emerging Infectious Diseases* after the
remaining EID modeling-compliance analyses are complete.

This manuscript reports a reproducible public-surveillance benchmark for
reported hantavirus incidence in EU/EEA countries, 2019-2023. Its public-health
contribution is not a deployable prediction system. The contribution is a
calibration-aware evaluation showing that sparse annual public data do not
support strong covariate-driven forecasting claims and that simple
surveillance-history baselines remain difficult to beat.

The final submission should emphasize four contributions relevant to EID
readers:

1. Exact reconciliation of 2019-2023 ECDC annual hantavirus totals across 142
   EU/EEA country-years.
2. Probabilistic evaluation using weighted interval score, 90% empirical
   coverage, interval width, mean absolute error, and Brier score.
3. Transparent negative or mixed results showing that public demographic,
   land-use, climate, and source-quality covariates did not produce stable,
   calibrated improvement over surveillance-history baselines.
4. EID-specific sensitivity, influence, calibration-localization, and
   detectability analyses that identify surveillance standardization and
   uncertainty reporting as practical constraints.

The manuscript does not claim operational risk prediction, causal environmental
drivers, individual-level risk, within-country risk, or generalizability beyond
EU/EEA annual reported surveillance. All analyses are retrospective. No
individual-level, clinical, address-level, or restricted data were used;
institutional review was not required.

All analysis code, processed public-data tables, and reproducibility scripts are
archived at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20150542. Before final submission,
the DOI will be verified against the final clean archive version. Source data
are from ECDC, World Bank, FAOSTAT, TerraClimate, and Natural Earth.

The manuscript has not been submitted elsewhere. The author has no competing
interests and received no external funding for this work. The author will
confirm final approval at submission.

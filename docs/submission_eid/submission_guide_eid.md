# EID Submission Guide

Use this checklist for the actual *Emerging Infectious Diseases* upload.

## Submission Target

- Journal: *Emerging Infectious Diseases*
- Article type: Research
- Manuscript title: Public Surveillance Benchmark for Reported Hantavirus Incidence, EU/EEA, 2019-2023
- Running head: EU/EEA Hantavirus Surveillance Benchmark
- Repository/archive DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20150542
- Clean local archive for a new Zenodo version: `hantavirus_benchmark_eid_submission_2026-05-13.zip`

## Files to Upload

- Main manuscript: `docs/submission_eid/manuscript_eid.docx`
- Cover letter: `docs/submission_eid/cover_letter_eid.docx`
- Main Figure 1: `docs/submission_eid/figures/Figure_1.tif`
- Main Figure 2: `docs/submission_eid/figures/Figure_2.tif`
- Supplementary appendix: `docs/submission_eid/supplements/Appendix_methods_eid.docx`
- Author statements: copy from `docs/submission_eid/author_statements.docx` into the submission forms if requested
- Final QA record: `docs/submission_eid/final_qa_report_eid.md` for your own reference, not for upload unless requested

Do not upload `overleaf_bundle/`, `overleaf_bundle.zip`, or any old package whose title says "predictor," IJHG, or Eurosurveillance.
If you update Zenodo before submission, upload `hantavirus_benchmark_eid_submission_2026-05-13.zip` as a new version and use the DOI Zenodo provides for that version.

2. Start a new manuscript submission.
3. Select article type: Research.
4. Paste the title, running head, abstract, one-sentence summary, and keywords from the manuscript title page.
5. Enter author:
   - Name: Julian Juan
   - Affiliation: Independent researcher
   - ORCID: 0009-0003-7234-2245
   - Email: bubgaming3@gmail.com
6. Enter your full mailing address and phone number in the portal and, if the portal requires it on the title page, add them to `manuscript_eid.docx` before upload. These personal fields cannot be inferred safely from the repository.
7. Upload the Word manuscript, cover letter, and separate TIFF figure files.
8. In the checklist/author questionnaire, disclose AI assistance exactly as stated in `author_statements.docx`.

## Final Human Checks

- Open `manuscript_eid.docx` in Word and confirm line numbers are visible.
- Confirm the title page is not anonymous.
- Confirm the abstract is unstructured and <=150 words.
- Confirm main text is <=3,500 words.
- Confirm tables are editable Word tables, not images.
- Confirm figures are separate TIFF files and are not AI-generated.
- Confirm the Zenodo DOI resolves.
- Confirm no local credential files or old ZIP archives are uploaded.

## Critical Blockers

### Data
1. **CDC WONDER bulk data access denied** — manual query-only, no API for automated county-level time series
2. **NEON small mammal data has seasonal gaps** — 6-month winter gaps at many sites break temporal continuity
3. **NEON seroprevalence too sparse** — 296 positives across 46 sites × 5 years = ~1.3/site/year, insufficient for training
4. **SafeGraph mobility data application rejected** — no alternative for human mobility edges in ST-GAT
5. **MODIS cloud contamination >30%** in Southwest monsoon season corrupts NDVI signal
6. **ERA5 soil temperature April data not available at county granularity** — 0.25° grid too coarse for local prediction
7. **Human case underreporting bias** — 85% subclinical in Europe means observed cases are a non-random sample
8. **No ground-truth rodent population density** — carrying capacity K(t) is entirely unobserved, only inferred

### Technical
9. **torchdiffeq ODE solver diverges** — stiff SEIR system with climate-driven K(t) causes NaN gradients
10. **TimesFM-2.5 not publicly available** — Google has not released weights; Chronos fallback may be insufficient
11. **PyTorch Geometric CUDA compatibility** — torch-scatter/torch-sparse wheels often break with new CUDA versions
12. **4× A100 requirement unrealistic** — most academic labs have 1-2 GPUs; DDP training fails on single GPU
13. **Gradient pathologies in PINN** — physics loss dominates early, data loss never recovers; adaptive lambda tuning is fragile
14. **ST-GAT over-smoothing** — 2-layer GAT collapses node representations to indistinguishable embeddings
15. **Negative binomial likelihood numerically unstable** — phi → 0 causes division by zero in loss
16. **Conformal prediction coverage collapses** — 90% PI achieves 60% empirical coverage on held-out El Niño years

### Scientific
17. **R0 derivation assumes homogeneous mixing** — real rodent populations are spatially structured, violating SEIR assumptions
18. **Sex-structured SEIR not identifiable** — male/female parameters (β_m, β_f, γ_m, γ_f) are highly correlated; cannot distinguish from single-sex model with data
19. **2-year climate lag is speculative** — no causal identification; could be spurious correlation
20. **Synthetic SEIR trajectories mis-specified** — real rodent demography includes seasonality, predation, and competitor species not in the model
21. **Spatial CV fails** — model trained on Southwest generalizes poorly to Northeast (different reservoir species, different climate drivers)
22. **Diebold-Mariano test underpowered** — only ~60 county-months with outbreaks in test set; p-values unreliable
23. **WIS improvement not clinically meaningful** — 0.42 vs 0.65 WIS sounds good, but both correspond to "predict ~0.5 cases/month with wide intervals"

### Publication
24. **Journal rejects for lack of real-world validation** — no prospective forecasting, only retrospective; reviewers demand operational deployment evidence
25. **Nature Communications desk rejects** — no experimental validation, no new biological discovery, purely methodological
26. **Reviewer demands human-to-human ANDV module** — cruise ship outbreak makes this politically salient, but ANDV parameters are essentially unknown
27. **Ethics/IRB concerns** — county-level risk maps could stigmatize rural/poor communities; journals may require ethics review

### Operational
28. **12-week timeline is fantasy** — data acquisition alone takes 4-6 weeks (NEON API rate limits, NASA Earthdata registration delays)
29. **Docker image >10GB** — pushes/pulls are slow; deployment to cloud costs $500+/month for GPU instances
30. **Real-time inference requires continuous data pipeline** — MODIS/ERA5 updates lag 2-8 days; "6-12 month forecast" is actually a batch retraining problem
31. **No public health partner buy-in** — CDC has no incentive to adopt an academic model; project dies after publication

### The Real Killer
32. **The model doesn't actually predict hantavirus** — it predicts *rodent seroprevalence proxies correlated with hantavirus risk*, and the correlation is weak (R² ~ 0.15 in published studies). You are building a very expensive rodent-density predictor, not a hantavirus predictor.

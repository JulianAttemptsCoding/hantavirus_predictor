## Possible Blockers for HANTA-PINN

### Data Blockers
- **NEON serology ends 2019** — no ground-truth rodent seroprevalence after 2019; model cannot be validated on recent reservoir dynamics
- **NEON geographic bias** — 46 sites concentrated in western/midwestern U.S.; may not generalize to eastern ecosystems or South America
- **No real-time rodent surveillance pipeline** — operational deployment requires NDVI/precip proxies that drift from true seroprevalence over time
- **CDC case underreporting** — human cases are sparse and biased toward high-profile outbreaks; spillover decoder validation is weak
- **Climate nonstationarity** — historical NDVI→rodent→case relationships may shift under climate change; model trained on 2014–2019 patterns

### Technical Blockers
- **PINN training instability** — ODE residual gradients can explode or vanish; physics weight annealing may fail to converge
- **Autograd through time** — `torch.autograd.grad` on the SEIR system is memory-intensive and may OOM on consumer GPUs
- **Stiff ODEs near threshold** — when R₀ ≈ 1, the system becomes numerically stiff; RK45 may fail or require tiny step sizes
- **Hyperparameter sensitivity** — PINNs are notoriously sensitive to the physics/data loss weight balance (α_physics)
- **Multi-GPU training complexity** — gradient checkpointing + ODE autograd + DDP is non-trivial to debug

### Validation Blockers
- **Cannot validate on human cases** — ~30 cases/year means statistical power for spillover forecasting is extremely low
- **No held-out epidemic** — no major hantavirus outbreak occurred 2019–2026 in the training domain to test predictive skill
- **Spatial CV may overestimate** — NEON domains are ecologically similar; leave-one-out may not test true generalization
- **Ablation studies need compute** — 7 ablations × 5 ensemble seeds × 3 CV folds = 105 training runs (~600 GPU-hours)

### Reviewer Blockers
- **"Not enough data for deep learning"** — expect pushback on 14K samples for 250K parameters despite physics constraints
- **"Why not just use SARIMA?"** — reviewers may question whether PINN complexity is justified over simpler methods
- **"No prospective validation"** — retrospective validation on 2014–2019 may be dismissed as curve-fitting
- **"Andes virus not modeled"** — the 2026 cruise ship outbreak makes H2H omission more visible

### Operational Blockers
- **Inference requires live climate feeds** — operational deployment needs automated MODIS/ERA5 ingestion pipelines that don't exist
- **Public health trust** — officials may reject black-box predictions regardless of uncertainty intervals
- **Regulatory** — publishing human health predictions may trigger FDA/CDC scrutiny or require IRB review

### Timeline Blockers
- **Data download is slow** — ERA5 CDS API has queue limits; MODIS bulk download is ~50GB
- **Synthetic pretraining is compute-heavy** — 10K trajectories × ODE solve = ~24 CPU-hours before neural training begins
- **Hyperparameter search** — Optuna with 100 trials × 500 epochs = ~2,000 GPU-hours for full tuning
